How to play

  • Coins ride the conveyor from left to right.
  • Drag a coin into a stack tube. Each stack can only hold one value.
  • Fill a stack to 6 coins to cash out and clear it.
  • Holding uncashed 10 coins increases Greedy Tax. When it hits 100%, you lose a random stacked coin.
  • If coins reach the right edge, you lose. Retry instantly.
MVP goals: readable loop, good drag feel, escalating pressure. No sound / meta yet.
