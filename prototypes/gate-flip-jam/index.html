How to play

  • Capsules spawn on the left side and move automatically every beat.
  • Tap an arrow gate to rotate it clockwise.
  • Send each color into the matching exit on the right.
  • Wrong exits or blocked traffic cost a heart.
  • Deliver the target amount before time runs out.

MVP note: this is a minimal HTML prototype focused on the core loop: readable routing, quick taps, instant fail/success feedback, and mobile-friendly play.
