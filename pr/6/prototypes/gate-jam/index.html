Gate Jam

Swipe a lane left/right to feed matching exits. Don’t let a lane fully fill or it locks.

Level 1 · Moves: 0
How to play: Touch a lane and swipe left/right. All blocks in that lane shift by one cell. Match a block to its same-color gate on the right to score. You have a move limit. If a lane becomes completely full (no empty cell), it locks for 3 moves.
