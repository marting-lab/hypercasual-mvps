How to play
  • Drag hex tokens into the currently open nest.
  • Tap a nest’s rotate button to switch which nest is open.
  • Fill a nest to lock it (nice bonus!)
  • Clear all tokens before the timer hits 0.

Core loop: fast sorting + quick “rotate or place?” decisions.

MVP note: no monetization, no progression — just the loop.
