How to play

  • Select one of the 3 grocery pieces.
  • Tap Rotate if you want to turn it.
  • Tap a board cell to place the shape with its top-left corner there.
  • Fill a full row or column to clear it.
  • Match frozen items to cold slots or dry items to dry slots for bonus points.
  • If none of the current pieces fit, the run ends.
