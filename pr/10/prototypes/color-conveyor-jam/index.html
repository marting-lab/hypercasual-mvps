Color Conveyor Jam — HTML MVP
Drag a block to choose a direction. It slides until it hits something. Conveyors then push blocks one step.
Level: 1/8
Moves: 0/18
How to play
  • Drag any block slightly to choose a direction (up/down/left/right).
  • The block will slide until it hits a wall or another block.
  • After you move, any block standing on a conveyor tile (arrow) gets pushed one cell.
  • Dock blocks into their matching colored exit squares to remove them.
  • Clear all blocks before the move limit.
MVP note: deterministic grid (no physics). Designed for quick iteration and UA-style “solve the jam” clips.
